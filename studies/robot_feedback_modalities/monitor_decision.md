# Recorded-trace screen before a live robot feedback comparison

Recorded after both fixed free-motion calibrations passed, before implementing this monitor or reading private contact labels for the six original episodes. Preserve the calibration prerequisite and every original episode outcome. This screen develops a mechanism; the observed starts are not held-out evidence for it.

Freeze three independent public-sensor signals. They are observations and candidate handoff triggers, not task decisions or physical success certificates:

- Changed wrist load: subtract the fixed empty-gripper reference fitted in `calibration_result.json`. Tare with the mean of up to three readings immediately before each waypoint. Flag a force change >= 5 N or torque change >= 0.5 Nm for three consecutive readings, after ten steps of that waypoint. These thresholds intentionally exceed ordinary small loads; they may miss gentle contact. Held-object inertia, gravity and purposeful contact remain confounds. Do not fit thresholds on labeled contact examples.
- Stalled requested motion: after at least ten steps, a target still outside 3 mm / 0.03 rad with less than 1 mm / 0.01 rad improvement over the last ten readings, plus low measured speed (0.005 m/s and 0.05 rad/s) for three readings. Require lack of progress on every still-unsatisfied component. A stall means unfulfilled motion, not confirmed obstruction or joint-limit diagnosis.
- Empty-sized closure: commanded gripper +1, opening <= empty-closed training maximum + 2 mm and finger speed <= 0.001 m/s for three readings after ten steps. This means empty or too thin to distinguish with this rule, not proof that a named object was missed. Any future stopping rule requires Astra to declare that it expects an occupied grasp; intentional empty closure must remain possible.

Apply each rule at 20 Hz using public pre/post measurements and original requested waypoint poses. Reset its history and tare at each waypoint; log every measurement, flag, first flag step and remaining requested/executed motion. Do not alter any original actions. Apply the same frozen rules to the full free-motion verification data too and report flags there, including transient dynamics; passing stationary compensation does not establish contact classification during motion.

Private saved-state geometry may contextualize signals: pad contacts with frame/tool, gripper contacts, proximal arm contacts and native assembly/hang transitions. Recomputed geometry is a contact-presence diagnostic, not a reconstructed force label. Do not pass private contacts, task checks or object identities to the monitor. Report whether signals precede the known empty-grasp, shallow seating and withdrawal events, and where they miss them or warn for intentional behavior. Retain all six trajectories, all waypoints and disagreements.

No predefined success-rate claim follows from this development screen. Select a small bounded stop/observe mechanism only if these public signals supply relevant timely information at a tolerable nuisance cost. Record that selection before live verification or a new protocol. Otherwise revise the mechanism rather than repeatedly tuning thresholds on these examples. Future policy comparison: two new matched starting states, fresh GPT-6 Astra medium contexts, identical calibration/processed sensor access/controller/budgets/computation, with every scheduled outcome retained.
