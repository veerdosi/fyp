## Hypothesis and scope

The official scaled-online CoTracker3 configuration may provide usable point identity and visibility where the frozen local-flow prototype drifted or missed loss. Its measured runtime is slower than real-time acquisition on this Mac, but that does not preclude a useful paused-simulation executor. First test geometric accuracy and loss at the actual causal update opportunities. Then, if supported, build a bounded feedback primitive and measure its whole-task tradeoffs against direct control on two new matched starts.

Retain all 212 pixel queries / 35 following-motion streams from all six original conditions and both observed starts. Use the pinned repository and checkpoint from `probe_protocol.json`, float32/MPS, official model resolution, 16-frame window, eight-frame step, six refinement iterations, six-by-six support grid and default 0.6 combined visibility/confidence threshold. Do not tune these settings or select successful-looking queries after results. Supply RGB/query pixels to CoTracker; use the original public metric depth/calibration for world unprojection. Object names, body geometry, native outcomes and private camera references remain scoring only.

## Causal availability and predefined metrics

Initialize each stream at its original query frame. The first prediction is available only once 16 chronological frames have arrived, at motion step 15. Later full updates add eight frames with overlap; retain a final partial update for every stream. Use only the latest endpoint prediction from each call. Never score earlier tracks revised with later images as though they were available earlier. No backward or offline predictor is permitted.

At these declared measurement opportunities, preserve the original numerical gates: accepted coverage of reference-visible points at least 80%, accepted current-point error p95 at most 3 mm, and false-valid rate on reference-occluded points at most 5%. Report every query, unknown geometric references and all original camera/motion/start categories. Missing categories or unknown references do not establish feasibility. Keep the previous 20 Hz local-flow audit distinct: this is a different, explicitly periodic measurement interface, not a claim that its old per-frame gate has passed.

Also retain a full 20 Hz availability ledger. Before bootstrap and between updates, label fresh measurement unavailable and record the age of the last endpoint, without fabricating a current point. Report fresh-frame coverage and bootstrap latency separately from endpoint accuracy. A future executor may pause or hold for fresh measurements; it may not silently use simulator truth or a stale estimate as fresh. Record synchronized wall time for each call, total tracking time, source/input hashes and every endpoint output. Predictions must not influence the recorded replay actions in this audit.

If precision, coverage or loss fails, preserve that result and diagnose which measurement/control scope is unsupported before any main robot comparison. If it passes, record the smallest sensor-driven command and its stop/unknown/deadline rules before implementation. Sol may run fixed verification motions to produce a replayable working primitive; Astra still chooses all evaluated grasps, targets, anchors, phase order and recovery. Freeze the subsequent two-start comparison, matched processed sensing and computation, shared native controller/budgets, success requirement and time/call/cost accounting before launching policy episodes. A working primitive is a milestone, not proof of a task benefit.
